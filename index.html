How it calculates

Each deposit goes in at the start of the year and grows once a year at your return minus fees. Withdrawals start the year after you stop adding, each one a fixed share of that year's opening balance, taken before growth. The ordinary-account comparison assumes a 3% dividend yield taxed at 20%, and 18% capital gains tax when you cash out. SARS annual limits follow the year of each deposit: R30 000 from March 2015, R33 000 from March 2017, R36 000 from March 2020 and R46 000 from March 2026, with a R500 000 lifetime limit and 40% tax on anything over.
